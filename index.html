About

About Piano Logger

Piano Logger is a browser-based piano MIDI visualizer and lifetime key logger. Plug in a MIDI keyboard, play with your computer keyboard, or click the on-screen piano — every note you play is saved on your own device, together with note, velocity, duration, time and input source.

Progress on an instrument is easy to feel and hard to see. By keeping a complete record of what you play, Piano Logger turns months of practice into something you can look back on: how much you played, which keys you favour, how your speed and dynamics change, and how consistently you showed up.

It combines a live visualizer with lifetime analytics, practice tools, a Voice Studio and a deep Theme Studio. There is no server and no account: your history, settings and recordings stay on your device, and you can export or back them up at any time.

Help & support

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Piano Logger?
A piano MIDI visualizer and lifetime key logger. Every note you play is saved on your own device, with statistics, charts, practice tools, Voice Studio and a large set of themes.
How do I connect a MIDI keyboard?
Open the app in Chrome, Edge, Brave or Opera on a desktop computer, click anywhere once to enable sound, plug in your keyboard and press Connect MIDI. Safari does not support Web MIDI. The Windows app handles MIDI without permission pop-ups.
Can I use my computer keyboard to play?
Yes. Tick Play with PC keyboard to play with two playable rows of keys (remappable). You can also click or tap the on-screen piano.
Does Piano Logger save my playing history?
Yes. Every key press is stored with its note, velocity, duration, timestamp and input source, and it stays until you remove it.
Where is my data stored?
Only on your own device. The website keeps it in your browser’s storage; the Windows app keeps its own data. There is no server and no account, and nothing is uploaded. Because the website and the app store data separately, move history between them with a backup. If you clear your browser data without a backup, the website’s history is lost.
How do I create a backup?
Go to Data → Create backup file. A Save dialog lets you choose where to put the file. Backups also carry your settings, themes and profile. The Windows app additionally makes an automatic backup every day into Documents\Piano Logger Backups.
How can I restore previous data?
Use Data → Restore and choose your backup file. This is also how you bring history from the website into the Windows app, or the other way round.
What is MIDI Keyboard Bridge?
A Windows-app feature that turns MIDI notes into computer-keyboard key presses so you can play Roblox Virtual Piano and similar games with a MIDI keyboard. It only presses ordinary keys, only in the window you choose and only while it is in front. Ctrl+Alt+End is an emergency stop. Keep an English (US) keyboard layout active in the game.
Does the desktop application work offline?
Yes. After the first download of each sampled instrument, the Windows app works fully offline. The website needs an internet connection to open.
What operating systems are supported?
The Windows app supports Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit; there is no 32-bit, ARM, macOS or Linux build. The web app runs in a modern desktop browser on any operating system (Chrome or Edge recommended).
How can I update Piano Logger?
The website is always current. For the Windows app, run the newest Piano-Logger-Setup.exe over the old one; your history, settings, themes and recordings are kept. If the installer offers “Uninstall before installing”, never tick “Delete the application data”. Make a backup first to be safe.
Why does Windows display an application warning?
The installer isn’t signed by a company, so Windows may say “Windows protected your PC”. Click More info → Run anyway. You can compare the installer’s SHA-256 checksum with the one published in the release.
